3. Marketing email

Marketing email is separate from using the Coach and off unless you choose it. You can choose it when you accept the terms, or later under your account in the Coach. You can turn it off there at any time, or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Sign-in codes and account emails are not marketing and are sent regardless.

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the Coach (Account… → Delete my account), confirmed with a code sent to your email. Deletion at once:

  • removes your email address, sign-in sessions, installations, marketing choice and pending codes;
  • signs you out on every Mac;
  • unlinks your usage records from you (they are then anonymous, and expire as above).

To prevent repeated free trials, we keep only a keyed fingerprint of your email address together with how much of your free trial was left, for 12 months. The fingerprint can't be turned back into your address. If you sign up again with the same address within that time, your trial continues from where it was instead of starting again. Records of your acceptance of the terms are kept as described in section 1, linked only to an anonymous number.

6. Security

Sign-in codes, tokens and network addresses are stored only as keyed fingerprints. Tokens on your Mac are encrypted. Our service holds the AI provider's key, so it is never on your Mac. Administrative access is limited to WSDA staff through Cloudflare's own sign-in.
