Privacy Policy

Effective [to be confirmed: effective date]

This Privacy Policy explains how Shields Innovations Inc., doing business as Walter Shields Data Academy ("WSDA", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, shares and retains personal information in connection with WSDA Software Coach: the macOS application, the WSDA online service it connects to, the installer download, and the Software Coach pages at wsdalearning.ai/software-coach (together, the "Service"). Other WSDA websites and products have their own notices.

What the app captures from your screen and sends to the AI model is described in detail in the AI Data Statement, which is part of this policy.

In short: we collect your email address to create your account and sign you in, records of your consent and of your AI usage, and only the technical information needed to run and protect the Service. Screen content and tasks pass through our service to the AI model and are not stored by WSDA. Marketing email is optional and separate from using the app. We do not sell the personal information we collect through the app.

1. Information we collect

Account and sign-in

  • Email address, which you provide to create your account and sign in.
  • One-time sign-in codes, stored only as a keyed cryptographic fingerprint, never as the code itself.
  • Sessions: a fingerprint of the sign-in token stored on your Mac, a random installation identifier created by the app (not a hardware identifier), and when the session was created and last used.

Consent and preferences

  • Your acceptance of the Terms and the AI Data Statement: which version, when, and the app version.
  • Your marketing email choice (yes or no) and when you made or changed it.

Free allowance and usage

  • Your free allowance: its size and how much is used.
  • Usage records for each AI request: when it was made, how long it took, its size in tokens, its cost, whether it succeeded, the app version and the AI model used. Usage records do not contain screen content, tasks or responses.

Content passing through the Service

  • Screen content and your tasks (screenshots, on-screen text, application and page details, your task, answers and notes, and the Coach's previous steps), as listed in the AI Data Statement. This content is sent through our service to the AI provider to generate guidance and is not stored by WSDA.

Security and abuse prevention

  • IP addresses are not stored. They are used only to compute keyed fingerprints that change daily and expire within 2 days, to limit abuse such as excessive sign-in attempts.

Stored on your Mac by the app (not sent to WSDA except as described in this policy)

  • Your sign-in token (encrypted with a key held in your macOS Keychain), your email address, the installation identifier, your app settings, and a local technical log. The log is sanitized and contains no screenshots, typed text or task content.

Website, download and marketing

  • Analytics and advertising measurement. The Software Coach web pages use Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics and the LinkedIn Insight Tag, which use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visit (such as pages viewed, referring page, campaign parameters, device and browser information, and IP address) and which buttons are used. The app itself contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • Optional email updates. If you sign up for Software Coach news on our website, we collect your email address, the time of signup and campaign information (the page, referrer and campaign parameters), and send them to Klaviyo.
  • Installer download. Downloads are served by Cloudflare. Our download service does not record information about visitors.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account, sign you in, and send sign-in and account emails;
  • provide the Service, including generating guidance from your screen content and task;
  • provide and enforce the free allowance and usage limits;
  • record your acceptance of our terms;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve the Service, using usage records (not screen content);
  • prevent fraud, abuse and repeated free allowances, and protect the Service and its users;
  • send marketing email only if you chose to receive it;
  • measure and improve our website and campaigns; and
  • comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not use your screen content or tasks to train AI models.

Marketing email is separate from using the app and is off unless you choose it. You can opt in when you accept the terms in the app, later in your account settings in the app, or by signing up on our website. You can opt out at any time in the app or by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Sign-in codes and account emails are operational messages, not marketing, and are sent whenever they are needed to provide the Service.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Account and deletion. You can sign out, sign out your other Macs, change your marketing choice, and delete your account in the app.
  • Marketing. Opt out in the app or by using the unsubscribe link.
  • Website tracking. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, use Google's opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics, and manage LinkedIn advertising preferences in your LinkedIn settings.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email walter@waltershields.com from the address associated with your account. We will verify your request by confirming control of that email address, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with your written permission. If we decline your request, you may reply to ask us to reconsider.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Account… → Delete my account), confirmed with a code sent to your email address. Deletion immediately signs you out on every Mac and deletes your email address, sessions, installation records, marketing choices, pending codes and free allowance, and removes the link between you and your usage records, which then expire on their normal schedule.

To prevent repeated free allowances, we keep only a keyed fingerprint of your email address and the unused part of your free allowance for 12 months after deletion. The fingerprint cannot be converted back into your email address. If you create an account with the same address within that time, your remaining allowance is restored instead of a new one being created. Consent records are retained as described in section 5. Marketing information held by Klaviyo is handled through its unsubscribe and our deletion process; to ask us to remove it, email walter@waltershields.com.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. These include storing sign-in codes, session tokens and network addresses only as keyed fingerprints, encrypting the sign-in token on your Mac with a key held in your Keychain, keeping our AI provider's key on our servers rather than in the app, and limiting administrative access to WSDA personnel through our hosting provider's authenticated access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at walter@waltershields.com and we will delete it.

10. Location of processing

WSDA is based in the United States. Our service and database are hosted by Cloudflare, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date. If a change affects what the app sends to the AI model, we will also publish a new version of the AI Data Statement, and the app will ask you to accept it before you continue.

12. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, email us at walter@waltershields.com. We handle privacy requests by email.
