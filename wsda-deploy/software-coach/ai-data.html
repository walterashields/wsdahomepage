AI Data Statement

Version 1 · Effective [to be confirmed: effective date]

This statement explains what WSDA Software Coach captures from your Mac, what it sends to an AI model, who processes that information, and what is kept. It is part of the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. The app asks you to confirm that you have read it before the Service starts.

1. How the Coach works

You describe a task. The Coach looks at the application you are working in and tells you, one step at a time, where to click or what to type and why. To do this, it captures information from your screen and sends it to an AI model that runs on our AI provider's servers, not on your Mac. The Coach does not click, type or act in your applications for you.

2. When the Coach looks

The Coach captures and sends information each time it checks your progress: when you start a task, after you act in the application, and when you select Look again or I'm stuck. It stops when you select Stop or the task ends.

3. What is sent

Each check sends the following through WSDA's online service to the AI model:

  1. A screenshot of the application you are working in: its front window, any open menus or pop-ups, and the macOS menu bar. The Coach's own windows are excluded. Content from other windows that overlaps the captured area can appear in the screenshot.
  2. On-screen text and controls that macOS Accessibility reports for that application, such as button names, menu items, labels and, where the application exposes it, text displayed in fields.
  3. The application's name and window title and, in a web browser, the address of the page.
  4. Your task in your own words, your answers to the Coach's questions, and any notes you add when you ask for help.
  5. The Coach's earlier steps in the current task, as text, and where you last clicked or scrolled (pointer positions and the control that was hit).

The Coach observes the positions of mouse clicks and scrolling to follow your progress. It never records keystrokes. It does not access your microphone or camera, files you have not opened on screen, or windows of other applications (except where they overlap the captured area). It sends no hardware identifier; a random installation identifier is used only for sign-in.

4. What is kept

  • On your Mac: each screenshot is sent once and discarded. Screenshots are not kept between checks or saved to disk. Earlier steps are kept only as text, for the current task.
  • By WSDA: our online service passes each request to the AI model and returns the response. It does not store screenshots, on-screen text, your tasks or the Coach's responses, and request logging is disabled for it. It keeps only usage records (when a request was made, how long it took, its size in tokens, its cost and whether it succeeded), which are deleted after 90 days.
  • WSDA does not use your screen content or tasks to train AI models.

5. Our AI provider

The AI model is Claude, provided by Anthropic, PBC, which WSDA uses through Anthropic's commercial API. Anthropic processes the information listed in section 3 to generate each response. Anthropic's commercial terms provide that it does not use customer inputs and outputs from its commercial API to train its models. Anthropic may retain inputs and outputs for a limited period, including to detect and prevent misuse, as described in Anthropic's own terms and policies. WSDA does not control that retention.

6. Your responsibilities

Because the Coach sees what is shown in the application you are working in, close, hide or avoid displaying information you do not want sent before you start or continue a task. This includes passwords, financial, health or government identification details, confidential business information, and other people's personal information that you are not permitted to share. Use Stop at any time to end screen checks immediately.

7. Your controls

  • Stop ends the task and all screen checks.
  • macOS permissions: the Coach requires Screen Recording and recommends Accessibility. You can turn either off in macOS System Settings at any time; the Coach cannot guide you without Screen Recording.
  • Account: you can sign out, or delete your account in the app (Account…). The Privacy Policy explains what deletion removes.

8. Changes

If we materially change what the Coach captures or sends, or who processes it, we will publish a new version of this statement, and the app will ask you to confirm it before the Service continues.
